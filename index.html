Blind math

A blind that arrives 2 cm too wide is a return, a wait, and a second delivery van. Blind math turns nine careful measurements into an inside-mount order size, counts the slats a venetian drop needs, and splits a wide span into blinds that fit the truck. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Inside-mount size - three widths, three drops and the maker's clearance become the order width and drop.
  • Venetian slats - drop, slat width and overlap become a slat count and the real coverage.
  • Split a wide span - one huge window becomes an even split of blinds under the width limit.

Labeled norms

Maker's clearance deduction defaults to 1 cm (common inside-mount practice; many makers take 0.5-1.5 cm). Slat overlap defaults to 0.5 cm. Widest single blind defaults to 180 cm (common roller limit; check your supplier). Results round to 0.1 cm. These are common trade norms, not manufacturer spec - your maker's numbers win.
